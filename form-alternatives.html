Use Option 1 (FormSubmit) on most free static hosts. It works on InfinityFree, 000webhost, GitHub Pages, etc.

Option 1: FormSubmit (Recommended - used in index.html)

First submission sends an activation email to alkay1093@gmail.com. Click the link in that email once to activate the form.

Option 2: Netlify Forms

Only works when the site is hosted on Netlify.

Option 3: Simple Mailto

Always works, but the user must send the email manually from their mail app.
